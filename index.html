Electromos Club Hardware

Dual-Axis Solar Tracker

Hardware prototype developed with the Electromos electronics club at IIT Bhilai. Dynamically steers a photovoltaic panel along azimuth and elevation axes to track peak solar incidence.

Arduino / C++ LDR Sensor Quad Dual Micro Servos Embedded Control
Engineering Highlights:
  • Differential analog comparison algorithm filtering sunlight noise.
  • 30% to 40% energy harvest gain over static planar installations.
  • Low-idle power sleep states to prevent auxiliary battery depletion.
